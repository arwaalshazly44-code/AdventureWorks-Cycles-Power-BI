# Key Business Findings & Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

### 1.1 Revenue Trends

**Findings:**
- 2020 Revenue: 6.40M
- 2021 Revenue: 9.32M
- 2022 Revenue: 9.18M

**Business Insight:**

Revenue increased significantly from 2020 to 2021, while 2022 recorded slightly lower revenue than 2021. However, since 2022 contains only six months of data, a direct comparison between the two years may be misleading.

### 1.2 Monthly Profitability

**Findings:**
- June 2020 Profit: 270.07K
- June 2021 Profit: 227.75K
- June 2022 Profit: 771.58K
- June 2022 recorded the highest monthly profit during the first six months of 2022.

**Business Insight:**

June 2022 achieved the highest profit among the analyzed months, supported by increases in both revenue and profit margin compared with the other months in the same period.

This highlights the importance of analyzing revenue and profit margin together when evaluating business performance.

### 1.3 Sales by Category

**Findings:**
- Bikes generated the highest revenue and profit.
- Accessories recorded the highest sales volume, with 58K units, representing 68.68% of total quantity sold.
- Despite having the highest sales volume, Accessories generated less profit than Bikes.

**Business Insight:**

High sales volume does not necessarily translate into higher profitability. Bikes contribute more to the business financially, while Accessories drive a larger share of product volume.

This highlights the importance of evaluating product categories using both sales volume and financial performance.

### 1.4 Sales by Country

**Findings:**
- The United States recorded the highest revenue, profit, and order volume.
- Australia recorded the highest Average Order Value (AOV).

**Business Insight:**

The United States is the strongest market in terms of overall sales contribution, while Australia has the highest average spending per order.

This suggests that market performance should be evaluated using multiple metrics, rather than revenue alone.

### 1.5 Workday vs. Weekend Orders

**Findings:**
- Workdays accounted for 71.77% of total orders (18K orders).
- Weekends accounted for 28.23% of total orders (7K orders).
- After accounting for the number of days in each group (5 workdays and 2 weekend days), the average daily order share was nearly equal:
  - Workdays: 14.35% per day.
  - Weekends: 14.12% per day.

**Business Insight:**

Although most orders occur during workdays, weekend activity is relatively strong when adjusted for the number of days in each group.

This suggests that customer ordering activity is distributed more evenly across individual days than the overall percentages initially indicate.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

### 2.1 Revenue Breakdown

The Decomposition Tree was used to explore revenue across multiple levels:

Year → Region → Category → Subcategory → Product → Customer.

This allows revenue to be investigated from an overall business level down to individual products and customers.

### 2.2 Top Revenue-Contributing Customers

**Findings:**
- 2020: Rafael Xu
- 2021: Janet Munoz
- 2022: Franklin Xu
- Overall (2020–2022): Jordan Turner

**Business Insight:**

The top revenue-contributing customer varies across individual years, while Jordan Turner leads in overall revenue contribution across the analyzed period.

This highlights the importance of analyzing customer contribution over both individual years and the full reporting period.

## 3. Product Performance

### 3.1 Revenue and Profit by Product

**Finding:**
- Mountain-200 Black, 46 recorded the highest revenue and profit among the analyzed products.

**Business Insight:**

This product is a strong financial contributor, leading in both revenue generation and profitability.

### 3.2 Orders and Quantity Sold

**Finding:**
- Water Bottle – 30 oz. was among the leading products in both order volume and quantity sold.

**Business Insight:**

High-demand products do not necessarily generate the highest revenue or profit. Evaluating products across multiple metrics provides a more complete picture of their performance.

### 3.3 Lost Sales by Product

**Finding:**
- Sport-100 Helmet Red recorded the highest lost sales among the analyzed products.

**Business Insight:**

The business should investigate the reasons behind the high lost sales for this product to identify potential improvement opportunities.

## 4. Returns Analysis

### 4.1 Overall Returns Performance

**Key Metrics:**
- Return Quantity: 1,828
- Return Orders: 1,809
- Lost Sales: 765.28K
- Returned Products: 124

### 4.2 Returns by Product

**Findings:**
- Water Bottle – 30 oz. recorded the highest return quantity.
- Sport-100 Helmet Red recorded the highest lost sales.

**Business Insight:**

The product with the highest return quantity is not necessarily the product with the highest financial loss. Therefore, return volume and financial impact should be evaluated separately.

### 4.3 Returns Trends Over Time

**Findings:**
- Return quantity increased from 2020 to 2022.
- Lost sales also increased over the same period.
- 2022 recorded the highest return quantity, return orders, and lost sales.

**Business Insight:**

The increasing trends in return quantity and lost sales suggest a potential issue that requires further investigation. Identifying the products contributing most to these trends can help prioritize improvement efforts.

### 4.4 Further Investigation

- Compare return quantity with total orders to calculate the return rate.
- Investigate the reasons behind the high lost sales of Sport-100 Helmet Red.
- Identify which products contributed most to the increase in returns over time.
## 5. Data Limitations

- The 2022 dataset covers only six months (January–June).
- Comparisons between 2022 and full-year periods should be interpreted carefully.
- The increase in return quantity does not necessarily indicate a higher return rate. Further analysis is needed by comparing returns with total orders.
- The reasons behind lost sales and product returns have not been established and require further investigation.
